About GYONGS

GYONGS is a modern fashion / lifestyle e-commerce brand for this school project. We sell trousers, shoes, accessories and a women's collection. The GYONGS logo is fictional and not connected to any real brand.

Brand concept

Clean streetwear: everyday trousers, sneakers, caps, bags and women's fits. Simple catalog, honest Rand prices, easy cart and checkout after login.

What we sell

Trousers • Shoes • Accessories • Women's Collection.

Values

Clear prices, real cart saved per user, orders saved in PostgreSQL, and a calm shopping experience you can explain in class.
